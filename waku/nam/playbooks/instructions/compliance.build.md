# compliance.build: Build the compliance requirements

FRS: F26
Stage: S5
Gate: every requirement decided

## Purpose

Build the compliance requirements the user department endorses: MSE preference, Make in India, OEM authorisation and the technical and department requirements.

## Steps

1. Propose the category's requirements from policy/compliance.json (DRAFT).
2. The department decides each one and may add its own (answer `requirements`).
3. Store the decided list as a .docx in the case's documents.

## Rules

- Beside the MSE, PP-LC and PTR-relaxation requirements, NRL's issued wording is shown verbatim with its tender (`boilerplate`, authority precedent): evidence for the department, never the requirement itself.

- Not applying a requirement, or adding one, needs its reason.
- NAM builds the requirements; NIT applies them.

## Tools

- `boilerplate`

## Outputs

- Each requirement, whether it applies and why.
